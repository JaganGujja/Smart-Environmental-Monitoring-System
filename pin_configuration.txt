ESP32 PIN CONFIGURATION
=======================

DHT22 DATA      -> GPIO 4
MQ-135 AO       -> GPIO 34 (ADC)
LDR divider     -> GPIO 35 (ADC)
OLED SDA        -> GPIO 21
OLED SCL        -> GPIO 22

DHT22 VCC       -> 3.3V
DHT22 GND       -> GND
OLED VCC        -> 3.3V
OLED GND        -> GND

IMPORTANT:
ESP32 ADC inputs must not receive more than 3.3 V.
MQ-135 modules are commonly powered from 5 V and their analog output
may exceed the ESP32 ADC input range. Use an appropriate voltage divider
or level-shifting circuit before GPIO34.
